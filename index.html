The Problem: Commuting Across VIT Vellore

With a 372-acre campus and 10-minute class breaks, shuttle transit has major pain points.

⏱

Unpredictable Wait Times

Students wait at stops without knowing if the shuttle just left or is stuck at a junction. Long delays lead to missed classes.

Average 15-20 min wasted
📍

Unknown Stops & Routes

Multiple shuttle loops run at once (Hostels to SJT, TT to Main Gate). Without route visibility, students board the wrong shuttle.

Confusion over destinations
🚦

No Moving / Stopped Status

Static schedules fail during rush hours. Students cannot tell if a shuttle is active on the road or stationary at a depot.

No live visibility

Campus context: walking from Men's Hostel Block-Q or the Ladies' Hostel to Silver Jubilee Tower (SJT) takes nearly 18 minutes.

How We Are Going to Proceed

Structured engineering timeline from research to live testing.

Phase 1 · Weeks 1-2

Campus Survey & Stop Mapping

Walk campus routes and record precise GPS coordinates for all 18+ stops (SJT, TT, MH, LH) and road junctions.

Phase 2 · Weeks 3-4

Driver GPS Telemetry Setup

Configure a lightweight driver beacon app streaming location and speed to the real-time database.

Phase 3 · Weeks 5-6

ETA & Motion State Logic

Implement geofencing around stops, ETA calculation algorithms and moving/stopped state detection.

Phase 4 · Weeks 7-8

Student App UI & Campus Trial

Build the student mobile interface, run a live test on 1 shuttle (MH-SJT loop) and collect feedback.
